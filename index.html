02 / About

We’ve sat on every side of the table.

We partner with founders to create, grow, and sell eCommerce, SaaS, and services companies.

We’ve founded companies, scaled them, invested in them, led acquisitions, and sold our own. That full view, from first hire to final close, is what we bring to every founder we work with.

Some founders want capital and a partner who has actually run a business. Others are preparing to sell and want someone in their corner who knows how buyers think, because we’ve been the buyer. Most need both at different points. We work on the problems that decide how a company turns out: positioning, hiring, distribution, and when the time comes, the exit.
